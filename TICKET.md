# STORE-412 — Store: buying an item

The storefront and player balances are already in production. We have ~2M daily
active players, around 85% of them on mobile. Now let players actually buy.

**As a player** I want to buy an item from the storefront with coins.

**As a game designer** I want to change prices in the admin panel and see players
pay the new price.

**As a producer** I want to run weekend sales announced with a push to the whole
audience.

**As support** I want to see what exactly a player spent their coins on, so I can
close "my coins disappeared" tickets.

**As a client developer** I want to know why a purchase failed, so I can show
either "top up" or "try again later".

Refunds and inventory are out of scope.
